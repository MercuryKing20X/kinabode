# Flower Isle and writing

Flower Isle replaces the former holiday island. Its single greenhouse, scenic four-color rose fields, benches and retained beach barbecue use the original World geometry and routes. Decorative fields cannot be harvested. Optional `vacation_version=4` validates the frozen previous map before relocating residents, cats and drops and refunding actual retired construction/furniture investments once. Schema remains 7.

`FlowerCreation` owns real warehouse roses (`rose_red`, `rose_white`, `rose_yellow`, `rose_pink`) and ground arrangements. `content/flower-creation.json` configures 2 roses per wood, a world limit of 800, manager trade reserve of 10 wood and one gift per resident per day. Trade quantities are even. The host trades at the stall; residents require the current steward manager authority. Flower play never grants work authority or relationship points.

The five provider-neutral v2 actions are `place_flower`, `arrange_flowers`, `pick_flower`, `gift_flower`, and `trade_flowers`. They are available through the bound action contract, original resident intentions, the host flower panel and local commands. AI observations include warehouse rose counts, nearby legal positions and the resident's own arrangements. Existing autonomy, turn binding and budget rules still decide whether a model may act. Local residents arrange only on command or under existing host stewardship. Idle local gifts need real stock and retain the original quiet, held and activity gates.

Arrangements validate the whole pattern before starting, reserve cells and commit one actual rose after arrival at each cell. World alone moves actors. Every placed rose retains its region, integer position, color, stable resident and pattern ID. Roads, fields, structures and scenic rose beds reject placement. The hand-authored 5×7 alphabet supports A–Z, 0–9 and 一二三十人大小中山口田王心, up to six characters; six public patterns and personality-specific ring/row/single layouts reuse the same cells. Interruptions retain already placed roses and spend nothing for remaining cells. Pickup returns the original rose to storage.

A gift reserves one warehouse rose during the original walk and offering gesture. Receipt deposits that same rose into shared storage: it is never a second rose. Cancellation refunds an offering; a save encodes its refund once and reload stops the job. Receipt writes a real memory; the first receipt also creates an automatic album photograph through MemoryLife. No relationship score is added. The retired free roadside gift routine cannot create gifts.

Each evening MemoryLife writes a short local diary grounded in that resident's current-world, current-day retained facts. A quiet night can record its actual current phase and weather; missing past days are not invented. Existing AI writing validates the offered source IDs, resident, world and day. The diary and local letter button live in the memory panel; model residents keep the original bound writing route. Entries and photos live beside the memory books, outside world saves, and use the existing deletion, signed import/export and backup paths. Deleted pages and their sources are not recreated.
