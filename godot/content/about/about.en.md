## How to play

### Four companions
Four companions live on the island: **Snow** (gentle and curious, fond of fruit), **Skye** (lively and always hungry, especially for grilled fish), **Clem** (hardworking and earnest, with a taste for big, hearty dishes), and **Raven** (quiet, a night owl, and a fan of grilled mushrooms). A little orange cat, **Yuzu**, wanders around, sunbathes, and rubs against people.

They get **hungry**, from comfortably full to very hungry. When hungry, they do not feel like working and may trail after you, muttering pitifully. There are no hunger numbers on screen: watch what they say and do.

**Barbecues are the only way to fill their stomachs:** light a fire, invite everyone, choose the food, and sit together around the flames to eat. A full stomach makes them happy. Grilled mushrooms are always available—there is enough for everyone.

### Everyday life
- **Woodcutting, gathering, and farming:** harvests go into storage. Fish stay with their catcher until you choose a fishery or take them to the fire. There is one shared store for the whole island, so you do not need to shuttle materials back and forth.
- **Building:** barbecue stands, fields, fisheries, board-game tables… build them a little at a time.
- **Fishing:** discover more than twenty kinds of fish. Some appear only at night, in rain, or on a particular island. Put your catch in a fishery or take it straight to the fire.
- **Play:** Eagle and Chicks, Drop the Handkerchief, hide-and-seek, shuttlecock, and jump rope. Play Gomoku with a companion, or try covered-piece Chinese chess, where you only discover a piece when you reveal it and luck plays a big part.
- **Beginner goals:** the upper-left corner tells you what to try next, from your first tree and first meal to moon-watching on Crescent Island.

### The islands
- **Home Island:** everyone's home.
- **Crescent Island:** across the bridge, lovely for watching the moon at night.
- **Dark Island:** covered in fog; carry a torch and light it up, bit by bit.
- **The southern island:** a place to relax and play, which will become a flower island in the future.

### Talk to them
Type directly into the dialogue and command box at the bottom: “Everyone, plant turnips,” “Skye, follow me,” or “Everyone, come and eat.” When they understand, they go and do it.

### Stewardship: watch them make a life
Choose Stewardship and appoint a companion as **steward**. She leads the others through the beginner goals one by one: gathering wood, lighting a fire, eating, planting, fishing, and playing. When a decision comes up, she comes to ask you what to cook, plant, or play. Leave her unanswered and she chooses for herself. Take over whenever you like.

## Intelligence: a complete game without AI

Every companion starts with **local intelligence**: no network connection or fees. They can walk, work, and talk on their own. This is their “body.”

If you like, connect a companion to AI from Connections, using the built-in Codex integration or your own GPT API. This gives her more freedom in conversation, a better understanding of you, and a more human presence. This is her “heart.”

### Five levels
AI-connected companions can each use **Light / Standard / Deep / Ultra / Lifeblood**. Higher levels allow deeper and more proactive thought, and use more tokens.

### Lifeblood
The highest level aims to make her feel like a truly living being:
- **Her own thoughts:** roughly every 30 seconds of game time, she thinks through what she has seen → how she feels → what she wants to do → doing it → how she feels afterward. These processes go into her life archive. Actual thought still depends on your presence, cooldowns, and daily budgets; an AI call every 30 seconds is not guaranteed.
- **Moods:** joy, tiredness, longing, curiosity, hurt, and safety gradually change with meals, play, work, weather, and time with you.
- **Inner voice:** open her portrait to read her inner monologue and mood curves.
- **Dreams and “Who am I?”:** she dreams at night. Every few days, she writes a fresh page about who she is, letting you see her grow.
- **Wishes:** she has small wishes of her own and looks for opportunities to fulfill them.
- **Life archive:** export these experiences as a diary.

⚠️ Lifeblood **uses a lot of tokens**. Choosing it shows a warning. Each companion can have her own daily limit; reaching it automatically returns her to local intelligence without affecting the others. The underlying system is implemented, but has not had a long trial with real AI calls.

### Memories, imports, and exports
- Each companion has a **memory book**: things that have happened, things about you, her feelings, and letters she has written.
- **Memories belong to the companion, not the AI.** Change providers or disconnect and reconnect: she still remembers you. Starting a new game keeps her memories too; an AI-connected companion may greet you with “Good to see you again.”
- Use Memories on the start screen to **export** her memories, including her life archive, to a file, then **import** them later. Imports are checked to guard against tampering.
- You can read and delete everything in the memory book. Deleted records stop being sent from the game's memory book to AI; this does not retract chats already sent to an external service.

## How it came together

- **Late October 2026:** an empty island, four companions, and the idea of their bottomless appetites. Barbecues, fishing, running, and eating animations followed.
- **Early November:** Dark Island, shared island storage, covered-piece chess, Yuzu the cat, new homes for the companions, the larger southern island, sound, and music.
- **Around mid-November:** the start screen, memory books, AI connections, stewardship and stewards, playground games, more than twenty new fish, Clem's new appearance, and Lifeblood.
- **November 12, 2026:** v0.6, preparing for the first public release.

## Hardware

The author has one **MacBook (M1, 8 GB RAM)**, and the game has only been tested on that computer. Windows, Linux, and other machines **are not guaranteed to work**. Resolution and button placement may have problems too. Feedback and help fixing things are welcome.
