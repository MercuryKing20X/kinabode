# Code of Conduct / 社区行为准则

We want a respectful, welcoming community for people of every background, identity, ability and level of experience. Be considerate, give useful feedback, and assume good intent while addressing harmful behavior directly.

我们欢迎不同背景、身份、能力和经验的人。尊重彼此、给出有帮助的反馈；可以讨论作品与观点，不针对个人攻击。

Harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual harassment, disclosure of private information and repeated unwanted contact are unacceptable. Do not post another person's credentials, private conversations or personal data.

不接受骚扰、威胁、仇恨言论、性骚扰、泄露隐私及反复违背意愿的联系。不得公开他人凭据、私聊与个人数据。

Report concerns to the maintainer through the contact in README (Douyin: MercuryFantasy). Include only the information needed to investigate; use a private channel for sensitive details. Maintainers will protect privacy, review reports fairly and may remove content, restrict participation or exclude contributors in proportion to the behavior.

请通过README联系方式向维护者报告。敏感内容使用私下渠道。维护者将保护隐私、公平处理，并视情况删帖、限制参与或移除贡献者。

This applies in project spaces and when representing the project. Good-faith disagreements and constructive technical criticism are welcome.
