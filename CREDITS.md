## Footsteps / 脚步

24 processed footstep files in `godot/content/audio/sound-v2/step.*.wav` derive from these CC0 recordings. Files were trimmed, processed and normalized by the project; exact authors and source-file hashes are in `godot/content/audio/sounds.json`.

- **TinyWorlds**, [Different Steps on wood, stone, leaves, gravel and mud](https://opengameart.org/content/different-steps-on-wood-stone-leaves-gravel-and-mud), [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- **Fantozzi**, [Fantozzi's footsteps: grass, sand, stone](https://opengameart.org/content/fantozzis-footsteps-grasssand-stone), [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

24份脚步由以上CC0录音裁切、处理和归一化；逐文件来源与原哈希见sounds.json。

Other shipped sound effects and character voice blips are project-authored procedural synthesis, not recordings or imitation of real people. Their original script/source parameters are recorded in the audio manifest.

其他音效与角色短音为项目原创程序合成，不是真人录音或模仿真人；来源参数在音频清单。

## Legacy rendered music / 旧音色音乐

21 OGG files under `godot/content/audio/generated/music.*` are retained legacy material and are not selected by the current runtime music catalog. The compositions are original; their **TimGM6mb** soundfont is by **Tim Brechbill (2004)** and **David Bolton (2010)**. The project's existing record conservatively labels the rendered recordings under GPL-2.0 as well; do not relabel them CC0 or MIT.

Retain [GPL-2.0](godot/content/audio/LICENSE-GPL-2.0.txt), the score/rendering tools, the [Debian copyright/source record](https://sources.debian.org/src/timgm6mb-soundfont/1.3-5/debian/copyright/), and corresponding soundfont source availability. These legacy files may be omitted from a smaller distribution only after confirming no retained tests or references need them.

旧21首OGG当前不被运行时音乐表引用；曲谱原创，TimGM6mb音色作者如上，项目既有来源将录音保守标GPL-2.0。保留GPL全文、谱与渲染工具、Debian来源说明及对应源材料可得性，不改称CC0/MIT。

## Artwork / 美术

Project artwork includes authorized AI-generated references/character/house/material sheets followed by background removal, cell extraction, pixel sampling, palette work, direction repairs and hand pixel edits. Original hand pixel matrices, code-baked deterministic props and crops/copies of earlier project artwork are identified separately in the file inventory. A mirrored or fallback frame is not claimed as newly drawn animation. Default independent editable character works remain separate from their action atlases.

美术如实区分授权AI生图后整理、原创像素矩阵、确定性原创道具与原像素派生；镜像/回退帧不冒称新画动作。人物可编辑源与动作图集保留独立。
